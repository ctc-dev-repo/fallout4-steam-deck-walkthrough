# fallout4-steam-deck-walkthrough

A beginner's walkthrough for **Fallout 4**, optimized for playing on the
**Steam Deck** — written for people who have never touched the game and want
maximum enjoyment per couch session.

- Full guide: [`WALKTHROUGH.md`](WALKTHROUGH.md)

## What's inside

- **Steam Deck setup** — 40 Hz/40 fps @ 8 W battery profile, the graphics
  toggles that matter (Weapon Debris off!), gyro aiming, and a grip-button
  Quicksave binding
- **Beginner guardrails** — recommended difficulty, a forgiving S.P.E.C.I.A.L.
  spread (Intelligence 5 = faster leveling), first-perk order, loot habits
- **Level-gated critical path** — Prologue → Concord Power Armor → minimum
  viable settlement → Diamond City → the Glowing Sea, spoiler-light
- **Spoiler-gated endgame** — faction comparison with a beginner
  recommendation (Minutemen path), clearly marked
- **DLC order** — Far Harbor at level 20+ as the must-play
- **UX habits** — 40–60 minute session structure, Boston-downtown stutter
  management, crash recovery

## Spoiler policy

Parts 1–7 of the guide are spoiler-light (objectives, not story reveals).
Part 8 contains major endgame spoilers behind a clear warning.
